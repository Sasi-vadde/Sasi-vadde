# Sasikanth Vadde

**Simulation Engineer, Alkaline Water Electrolysis and Energy Systems** · Stuttgart, Germany

Chemical and Energy Engineer (M.Sc., Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, grade 1.9) working where electrochemistry meets system simulation. At the DLR Institute of Engineering Thermodynamics in Stuttgart (10/2023 to 12/2025, from intern to Research Associate) I worked on transient Dymola/Modelica models of alkaline electrolysis cells and stacks, validated them against test-bench measurements, ran OpenFOAM thermal studies, and supported test-rig work (P&ID diagrams, equipment set-up, experiment analysis, test scripts). Employer work (DLR) is confidential and not shown here; projects below are coursework and personal.

Looking for simulation and modelling roles in hydrogen and energy systems in Germany.

### Focus

- Physics-based modelling of electrochemical systems (Dymola/Modelica): two-phase flow, gas crossover, thermal behaviour
- Model validation against measured data and root-cause analysis of model-vs-measurement mismatches
- CFD and conjugate heat transfer (OpenFOAM, STAR-CCM+)
- Process control: PID and multi-loop control for fluctuating renewable load
